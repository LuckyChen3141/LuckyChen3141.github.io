About me

An engineering foundation.
A focus on operations.

I go by Luke. I am a PhD student in Operations and Technology Management at the Questrom School of Business, Boston University. My research interests center on AI token pricing, alongside energy markets, electric vehicles, and sustainability. I approach these topics through optimization, market design, and incentives.

I began my doctoral studies at Boston University in Systems Engineering before moving to Questrom. Earlier, I earned a Bachelor of Engineering at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech) and spent a semester at the University of Toronto.

My research experience spans vehicle-to-grid energy trading, cybersecurity, integrated sensing and communication, and robotics. I am interested in connecting these technical foundations with questions about incentives, coordination, and sustainable mobility.
